Source Segregation is Every Citizen's Responsibility

By segregating your household waste into Wet, Dry, and Hazardous bins, you help 10,000+ Nagar Nigam sanitation workers stay safe, prevent overflowing landfills, and turn organic waste into clean compost.

The 3-Color Waste Segregation Rule (Mandatory under Municipal By-Laws)

Green Bin

Wet / Organic Waste

Biodegradable organic waste that can be converted into natural compost or bio-gas.

What to put in:

• Fruit & vegetable peels

• Cooked food leftovers, rice & rotis

• Tea bags & coffee grounds

• Garden dry leaves & flowers

• Egg shells & bones

Never put plastic polythene bags inside the green bin!

Blue Bin

Dry / Recyclable Waste

Inorganic items that can be recycled into new products at Material Recovery Facilities (MRF).

What to put in:

• Newspapers & cardboard cartons

• Plastic bottles (rinsed & crushed)

• Milk pouches (rinsed with water)

• Glass bottles & metal tin cans

• Tetrapak juice cartons

Always ensure items are rinsed clean and dry!

Red / Black Bin

Hazardous & Sanitary

Toxic chemicals, electronic waste, and sanitary items requiring specialized incineration.

What to put in:

• Expired medicines & syringes

• Batteries & cell phone batteries

• Diapers & sanitary pads (wrap in paper with red X)

• CFL bulbs & tube lights (mercury)

• Paint cans, chemicals & pesticides

Wrap broken glass in paper to protect sweepers.
